## 1. Volume Overview

Production volumes at BKK are measured in two primary units:
- **Cases** (standard case equivalent, as planned and reported in SAP)
- **Litres / kilograms of bulk** (for bulk make/mixing operations)

Volumes are planned on a **monthly basis** via the Integrated Business Planning (IBP) / S&OP process, broken down to weekly and daily schedules by the plant planning team.

| Metric | Unit | Data Source |
|---|---|---|
| Production Volume | Cases (CE) | SAP PP / MES |
| Bulk Volume | Litres / kg | SAP PP / Batch Records |
| Capacity Utilisation | % | Calculated (Actual / Net Available) |
| Volume Attainment | % | SAP PP vs. Production Orders |

## 5. Volume Trends and Seasonality

### Seasonal Demand Patterns

Hair care demand at BKK typically exhibits the following seasonal patterns:

| Period | Demand Pattern | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1 (Jan–Mar)** | Moderate | Post-holiday restocking |
| **Q2 (Apr–Jun)** | Low-to-Moderate | Regular replenishment |
| **Q3 (Jul–Sep)** | High | Back-to-school + festival season (Asia-Pacific) |
| **Q4 (Oct–Dec)** | High | Holiday season demand peak + year-end trade loading |

> **Note:** Actual demand patterns should be validated against the IBP demand signal and confirmed with the commercial/sales team.

### Key Volume Drivers and Risks

| Driver / Risk | Impact on Volume | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Promotional campaigns (trade promotions) | +10–25% volume spike for specific SKUs | Build safety stock ahead of promotion; model in IBP |
| New product launches (NPD) | Capacity allocation required for trials and initial runs | Reserve capacity in master production schedule |
| Regulatory changes (formula, labelling) | Potential rework or production hold | Engage QA and regulatory teams early in change management |
| Material shortages (RM/PM) | Reduced throughput or line stoppages | Supplier risk assessment; safety stock norms for critical materials |
| Equipment breakdowns | Unplanned downtime → volume loss | Preventive maintenance program; spare parts management |
