# SIP-BKK — BKK Hair Care Plant Knowledge Base

This space serves as a comprehensive knowledge base for the **BKK Hair Care plant**. It captures the plant's end-to-end operations, including production volumes, manufacturing lines, capacities, planning processes, constraints, losses, and other relevant operational information.

## Objective

The primary objective is to develop a complete understanding of how the plant operates and use this knowledge to support the transition toward **touchless planning**. The space also documents the value stream mapping (VSM) process, focusing on identifying operational losses, understanding their root causes, and defining action plans to eliminate or reduce them.

## How to Use This Knowledge Base

- **Plant Operators & Engineers:** Start with [Plant Overview](docs/01-plant-overview/README.md) and [Manufacturing Lines](docs/02-manufacturing-lines/README.md) for operational reference.
- **Supply Chain & Planning Teams:** Refer to [Production Volumes](docs/03-production-volumes/README.md) and [Planning Processes](docs/04-planning-processes/README.md) for planning data and touchless planning context.
- **Continuous Improvement Teams:** Focus on [Operational Losses](docs/06-operational-losses/README.md), [Value Stream Mapping](docs/07-value-stream-mapping/README.md), and [Action Plans](docs/08-action-plans/README.md).
